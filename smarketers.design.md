# The Smarketers — Deck Design System
*Derived from: Praeemineo Kick-Off Deck*

![The Smarketers](smarketers_logo.png)

A premium, warm-toned client-deliverable style. Soft peach/blush backgrounds, confident violet-purple accents, generous whitespace, and clean white cards. Reads as polished and modern rather than "corporate slide template."

---

## 1. Logo & Brand Mark

- **Mark:** six-node circular knot in a rainbow gradient (blue → green → yellow → orange → red → purple), forming a ring around a white center.
- **Lockup:** mark + "The Smarketers" in a light, geometric sans-serif (thin/regular weight, generous letter-spacing — note the wide tracking on "S m a r k e t e r").
- **On-slide usage:** small version of the lockup top-left of every slide — mark (~28–32px) + **"The Smarketers"** (bold, dark `#1A1A1A`) + tagline **"Your Growth Catalyst"** underneath in small italic gray. On dark/violet slides (e.g. closing slide), the wordmark switches to white; the colorful mark itself never changes.
- **Clear space:** keep the mark's full ring diameter as minimum padding on all sides. Never recolor or flatten the ring gradient.

## 2. Color Palette

### Backgrounds
| Swatch | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Cream | `#FFF4F0` | Default slide background (base) |
| Blush Pink | `#F3D9E3` / `#FCE9F0` / `#FBD9E6` | Soft radial glow / gradient overlays in slide corners |
| Dusty Mauve | `#C9A9B8` | Secondary gradient tone, also used as shadow tint |
| Pale Lilac | `#E7D6F5` | Light purple gradient accent on data-heavy slides |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Card / panel surfaces |

### Core Brand Accent (Violet–Purple)
| Swatch | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Violet | `#7C34BC` | Headline highlight words, secondary accent |
| Signature Purple | `#8B2CE0` | Primary accent — eyebrow labels, icon chips, links, numbering, closing-slide background |
| Magenta Pop | `#E8447A` | Rare high-emphasis accent (e.g. a stat number) — use sparingly |

### Text
| Swatch | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Near-Black | `#1A1A1A` | Headings, primary text |
| Slate Gray | `#3A3A40` / `#5B5B63` | Body copy, subtitles |
| Muted Gray | `#8E8E97` | Tertiary / de-emphasized text, captions |

**Rule of thumb:** every slide = cream/blush base + white cards + one purple accent family. Never introduce a competing hue (no blue, green, or orange) — Magenta is the only permitted "pop" color, used once per slide at most.

## 3. Typography

- **Typeface:** Arial (clean geometric sans). Headings and labels are always **bold**; body copy is regular weight.
- **Type scale:**
  - Slide title: **30–36pt, bold**, near-black, with the key phrase set in purple (`#7C34BC`/`#8B2CE0`) for emphasis — e.g. one clause in black, the pivotal phrase in purple.
  - Eyebrow / kicker label: **10–12pt, bold, uppercase, letter-spaced**, purple — sits directly above every title (e.g. "ENGAGEMENT OVERVIEW").
  - Subtitle / intro line: **14pt regular**, slate gray, directly under the title.
  - Card title: **14–16pt bold**, near-black.
  - Card eyebrow (inside a card): **10–11pt bold uppercase**, purple.
  - Body / list text: **10–12pt regular**, gray.
  - Footer / page number: **7.5–10pt**, muted gray, bottom-right corner ("06 / 18").
- **Numbering motif:** two-digit index numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`…) set bold and large in purple, used as the leading visual for workstreams, phases, and steps.

## 4. Layout & Spacing

- **Header zone:** logo lockup top-left, consistent position/size across all slides.
- **Content start:** eyebrow label → title → one-line subtitle, left-aligned, starting ~5–8% from the left edge.
- **Grid:** content mostly split into 2 columns of white cards (roughly 45/55 or 50/50), or a 3–4 column row of equal cards for step/phase sequences.
- **Margins:** minimum ~0.5" from slide edges; ~0.3" gaps between cards.
- **Footer:** slide number bottom-right, small and unobtrusive.

## 5. Components

**Cards / Panels**
- White fill, generously rounded corners.
- Soft drop shadow: dusty-rose tinted (`#C9A9B8` at ~35% opacity), offset down, wide soft blur — never a flat black shadow.
- Internal padding is generous; content never touches the card edge.

**Pills / Badges**
- Fully rounded (stadium-shaped), used for tags, meeting-info chips, and workstream labels (e.g. "Workstream 1 · Digital Foundations & Trust").
- White or tinted fill, bold small-caps-style text.

**Stat highlight cards**
- Small rounded cards with a tinted background (soft pink or soft lilac), a large bold number/word in the accent color, and a small uppercase label beneath (e.g. **"6" / MONTHS**, **"Parallel" / DELIVERY**).

**Icon chips**
- Small rounded-square "chip" in a tinted purple/pink background, holding a simple line icon (camera, chat, phone, document, etc.), paired next to a bold label.

**Checklist / list rows**
- Purple checkmark or bullet, bold lead-in text, regular gray supporting text — generous vertical spacing between rows rather than tight bullet stacking.

**Section divider / closing slide**
- Solid signature-purple background (`#8B2CE0`) with a soft light-to-magenta gradient glow washing across one side.
- All text flips to white; large bold statement headline ("Thank you."), a lighter secondary line, and an outlined semi-transparent pill for a final call-to-action/question.

**Bottom accent bar**
- A thin (~4–6px) gradient strip runs along the very bottom edge of every slide, transitioning through the purple → magenta accent range. It's the one recurring "signature" decorative touch — do not widen it into a full accent bar/stripe on the body of the slide.

## 6. Decorative Backgrounds

- Soft, diffuse blush/lilac glows in the upper corners of content slides — never sharp-edged, always a gentle gradient blend fading back into the cream base. These sit behind all content and never reduce text contrast.

## 7. Iconography

- Simple, single-weight line or solid icons (no multi-color or photorealistic icons).
- Always housed in a tinted rounded chip rather than floating bare.
- Icon color is either white-on-purple or purple-on-tint — always high contrast against its chip.

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Keep one purple family as the only strong accent per slide.
- Pair every title with a purple uppercase eyebrow label.
- Use white cards on the cream base for all structured content.
- Keep shadows soft and warm-tinted, never harsh black.

**Don't**
- Don't introduce blue, green, teal, or orange as UI accents — the logo mark is the only place the full rainbow appears.
- Don't use sharp drop shadows or hard borders on cards.
- Don't stack more than one "pop" magenta element per slide.
- Don't add heavy horizontal rule lines under titles — the eyebrow label + whitespace does that job.
